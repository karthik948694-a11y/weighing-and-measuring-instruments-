How the 3-tier compliance system works

Under Section 24 of the Legal Metrology Act, every commercial measuring device must be re-verified annually.

Valid

Active stamping approval

More than 30 days remaining on annual certification. Deviation tolerance verified under ±0.01%. Official seal intact.

Fully compliant for commercial retail and industrial trade.
Renewal due

Within 30-day window

Expiry date approaching. Business owner receives automatic reminders to schedule calibration inspection before cut-off.

Immediate renewal submission advised to prevent lapse.
Expired / violation

Non-compliant scale

Certificate expired. Using this instrument in commercial transactions attracts statutory penalties and potential machine seizure.

Subject to Section 24 penalty notice and legal prosecution.
